\section*{B1. Accounts and admin}
\small
\begin{longtable}{|p{1.1cm}|p{6.6cm}|p{6.3cm}|}
\hline
\textbf{ID} & \textbf{Steps} & \textbf{Expected result} \\
\hline
\endhead
S-A1 & Register a passenger with a USC email. Upload Study Load, Parent’s Permit and USC ID. Click the verification email. & Account shows as pending until an admin approves it. No ride features before then. \\
\hline
S-A2 & Register a car driver with Study Load, Parent’s Permit, OR/CR, Driver’s License, MVP Pass, a vehicle category and a fuel type. Repeat for a motorcycle with helmet proof. & Registration completes. Motorcycle drivers get gasoline only. \\

S-A3 & Try to register with a non-USC email. & Registration is refused with a clear message. \\
\hline

\hline
S-A5 & Open Profile on passenger, driver and admin accounts. & Only drivers see Ride Token balances and mood detection. Only passengers pick a mood preference. Only admins see reports and suspension tools. \\
\hline
S-A6 & Log in with an account whose email is not yet verified. & Login is blocked on the login screen. \\

\section*{B2. Ride flow}
\small
\begin{longtable}{|p{1.1cm}|p{6.6cm}|p{6.3cm}|}
\hline
\textbf{ID} & \textbf{Steps} & \textbf{Expected result} \\
\hline
\endhead
S-B1 & Passenger off campus requests a ride at an approved pickup point. Then try writing a request document directly with a client (rules emulator or a debug call). & The request exists with direction set by the server (inbound). The direct write is refused. \\
\hline
S-B2 & Driver goes Available, selects the pickup point, accepts the request. & Request becomes accepted, remaining capacity drops by one, and the passenger sees the driver’s name, vehicle and plate. \\
\hline
S-B3 & Driver starts the trip, confirms pickup, then completes it near the Bunzel Lobby. & Statuses advance accepted, picked up, completed. Passenger gets the arrival screen; driver gets the completion screen. \\
\hline
S-B4 & Send chat messages both ways during the ride, then again after completion. & Messages appear on the other device. Chat stays available for about a day after completion, then closes. \\
\hline
S-B5 & Outbound: both on campus. Driver picks an end destination by typing (Places search) and by dropping a pin. Passenger picks a drop-off point. & Driver sees drop-off points along the route with request counts. Passengers are grouped by shared drop-off point. \\
\hline
S-B6 & Passenger cancels before acceptance. Driver cancels an accepted passenger. Driver ends a started trip early with a reason. & Each path completes and the other side is told why. \\
\hline
S-B7 & Two passengers request the same pickup point at different times. One widens their preference after two minutes. & The earlier request is offered first. Widening keeps the original place in line. \\
\hline
S-B8 & Passenger rates the driver. Open both users’ ride history. & The rating saves and the ride appears in history with the right details. \\
\hline
\end{longtable}

\section*{B3. Matching filters}
\small
\begin{longtable}{|p{1.1cm}|p{6.6cm}|p{6.3cm}|}
\hline
\textbf{ID} & \textbf{Steps} & \textbf{Expected result} \\
\hline
\endhead
S-C1 & Place the driver 350 m from the pickup, then 600 m away, with a free seat. & Within 400 m the driver is notified and can accept. At 600 m there is no prompt and the server refuses an accept. \\
\hline
S-C2 & Fill the vehicle to capacity, then make a new request. & No prompt to that driver and the server refuses an accept. \\
\hline
S-C3 & Try to accept a request with the driver’s stored location more than 400 m from the pickup. & matchRide refuses it. \\
\hline
S-C4 & Put an inbound request’s driver on the wrong side of the campus fence. & matchRide refuses it on direction. \\
\hline
S-C5 & Passenger accepts only Quiet. Test a Lively driver, a Quiet driver, and a driver with no mood tag. & Lively driver is hidden and blocked. Quiet and unknown drivers match (the filter fails open). \\
\hline
S-C6 & Passenger prefers motorcycle. Test a car driver, a motorcycle driver, and “No preference”. & Car driver is blocked for a motorcycle-only passenger. “No preference” matches everyone. \\

\section*{B4. Tokens and fuel}
\small
\begin{longtable}{|p{1.1cm}|p{6.6cm}|p{6.3cm}|}
\hline
\textbf{ID} & \textbf{Steps} & \textbf{Expected result} \\
\hline
\endhead
S-D1 & Complete one trip. Check the reward ledger. Call the completion again. & Exactly one award. The client sent only a trip ID. A retry returns the same award. Check against the formula: fuel price $\times$ (distance $\div$ efficiency) $\div$ 5, rounded up to 0.01 (PHP 60/L, 13 km, 26 km/L gives 6.00). \\
\hline
S-D2 & Complete a trip with two or three passengers. & The first passenger gets the full value. Riders who travel the whole shared distance split equally. Others follow Equation~(\ref{eq:extra}). The breakdown on the admin card matches. \\
\hline
S-D3 & Set a semester period with the admin screen or the set-ces-period script, then push a driver’s total near 300. & The award is clamped at 300 per semester. The excess is forfeited, and the ledger shows the uncapped amount and the cap reason. \\
\hline
S-D4 & On Admin Fuel Price, tap “Refresh now”. & Prices and week update with a clean status. If DOE has no newer week, the stored price is untouched and the status says why. There is never a fallback price. \\
\hline
\end{longtable}
