\section*{Part 1. Location}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Where do you primarily live in the first semester of A.Y. 2026--2027?* (Cebu City, Mandaue City, Lapu-Lapu City, Talisay City, or Other)
    \item To help us identify potential pick-up or drop-off zones for the SanCarGo app, please tell us the nearest major landmark to where you start your daily commute or route (for example, Ayala Center Cebu, Banilad Town Centre, Talamban Gym, or a specific condominium building). Please do not enter your exact house number or street address. This information will be kept strictly confidential. (Short answer)
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Part 2. Your mobile device}
\begin{enumerate}
    \setcounter{enumi}{2}
    \item What operating system is your mobile device?* (iOS (Apple iPhone), Android (Samsung, Google, Huawei, Xiaomi, etc.), or I have both)
    \item What is your phone model?* (Short answer. Examples: iPhone 17, Samsung Galaxy S3, Honor, Oppo. If you have multiple, separate them with a comma.)
    \item What network carriers do you use for cellular/mobile data?* (Select all that apply: Globe, GOMO (TM); Smart (TnT/Sun); DITO; or Other)
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Part 3. User acceptance testing}
\begin{enumerate}
    \setcounter{enumi}{5}
    \item We are looking for a small group of student volunteers to pilot test the SanCarGo application for two weeks. Would you be interested in participating in this User Acceptance Testing (UAT) phase?* The form notes that participating as student-drivers can earn up to 14 CES points, depending on the number of days of participation. (Yes, I am interested in being a pilot tester; or No, I am not interested)
    \item Select which role you would like to be.* For those who choose the driver role, the form asks the student to make sure that the vehicle (car or motorcycle) has the USC Motor Vehicle Pass sticker, and notes that only student-drivers can earn CES points. (Both Driver and Passenger, or Passenger Only)
\end{enumerate}
After this part, the form tells the student that an email with the date and time of the onboarding for UAT will follow.

\section*{Part 4. For student-drivers}
\begin{enumerate}
    \setcounter{enumi}{7}
    \item Which vehicle has the USC Motor Vehicle Pass sticker?* The form notes that the study is limited to one registered vehicle per student-driver, so the student selects the vehicle to use for ridesharing. (Car or Motorcycle)
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{For car drivers}
The form states that, due to the scope and limitations of the research, student-drivers must own a gasoline-powered or diesel-powered vehicle.
\begin{enumerate}
    \setcounter{enumi}{8}
    \item What fuel does your car use?* (Gasoline or Diesel)
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{For motorcycle drivers}
\begin{enumerate}
    \setcounter{enumi}{9}
    \item Do you have a spare helmet that you can lend to a potential passenger?* (Yes or No)
\end{enumerate}
