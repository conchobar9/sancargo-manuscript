\chapter{Beta Testing and UAT Feedback Survey}
\label{app:survey}
\clearpage

One Google Form is sent to each tester after the tasks, and it shows each group only its own parts. Beta testers answer Parts 1, 2, 4, and 5. UAT participants answer Parts 1, 3, 4, and 5. Ratings from 1 to 5 mean 1 strongly disagree and 5 strongly agree.

\section*{Part 1. About you}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Tester code (the team gives you one, so no names are needed). \textit{Beta testers only.}
    \item How often do you use ridesharing apps? (Never, sometimes, or often) \textit{Beta testers and UAT participants.}
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Part 2. Task results (beta testers only)}
For every task you tried (T1 to T3, W1 to W11, and O1 to O3 if you did them): Pass, Fail, or Could not try. If Fail, a text box asks: ``What did you see instead?

\section*{Part 3. System Usability Scale (UAT participants only)}
Rate each statement from 1 to 5.
\begin{enumerate}
    \item I think that I would like to use SanCarGo frequently.
    \item I found SanCarGo unnecessarily complex.
    \item I thought SanCarGo was easy to use.
    \item I think that I would need the support of a technical person to use SanCarGo.
    \item I found the various functions in SanCarGo were well integrated.
    \item I thought there was too much inconsistency in SanCarGo.
    \item I would imagine that most people would learn to use SanCarGo very quickly.
    \item I found SanCarGo very cumbersome to use.
    \item I felt very confident using SanCarGo.
    \item I needed to learn a lot of things before I could get going with SanCarGo.
\end{enumerate}

Scoring: for odd items, subtract 1 from the answer; for even items, subtract the answer from 5; add the ten values and multiply by 2.5, which gives 0 to 100. The scores are averaged over the UAT participants. About 68 is the usual benchmark for average usability.

\section*{Part 4. Your experience (beta testers and UAT participants)}
Rate each statement from 1 to 5.
\begin{enumerate}
    \item The app responded quickly (loading screens, sending a request, accepting).
    \item Notifications arrived when I expected them.
    \item The error messages told me what to do next.
    \item The screens and the map were easy to read.
    \item The emergency help button was easy to find.
    \item I understood why the app asked for location access (and the microphone, for the driver).
    \item I felt my personal information was handled safely.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Part 5. In your own words (beta testers and UAT participants)}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Did the app crash, freeze or lose your place at any time? (Yes or no. If yes, when?)
    \item What was the most confusing part?
    \item What did you like most?
    \item What one change would most improve it?
    \item Would you use SanCarGo to get to or from campus? (Yes, maybe or no, and why.)
\end{enumerate}
