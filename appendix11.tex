\chapter{Names, Stored Values, and Data of the Mood AI}
\label{app:clips}
\clearpage

This appendix lists the names that the text uses for the mood AI and the values that the application stores, and it lists the files and the data sets of the mood AI with their licences. In the text, the display names are used. In the code, in tables of fields, and in this appendix, the stored values are used.
