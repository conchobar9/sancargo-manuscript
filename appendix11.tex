% Appendix: Nine Real-World Test Clips
\chapter{Nine Real-World Test Clips}
\label{app:clips}
\clearpage

The nine clips were played through a Bluetooth speaker near the microphone of the device in the app, and each has an expected mood that was set before testing. Table~\ref{tab:ai-clips} lists the clips and what was observed. Correct means that the mood given by the model, or the tag displayed by the app on the emulator and the devices, matched the expected mood. Incorrect means that it did not. Songs 1 to 9 are numbered as in Section~\ref{sec:ai-ondevice}.
